# Hadith — arabic | translation | reference
الصَّلَوَاتُ الْخَمْسُ كَفَّارَةٌ لِلذُّنُوبِ مَا لَمْ تُؤْتَىٰ بِالْكَبَائِرِ | The five prayers are an expiation for sins, as long as major sins are avoided. | Sahih Muslim
مَثَلُ الصَّلَاةِ كَنَهَرٍ عَذْبٍ يَغْتَسِلُ فِيهِ الْإِنسَانُ | The example of the five prayers is like a flowing river in which one bathes five times a day. | Ibn Hibban
أَحَبُّ الْأَعْمَالِ إِلَى اللَّهِ الصَّلَاةُ فِي وَقْتِهَا | The most beloved deed to Allah is prayer at its proper time. | Sahih al-Bukhari
بَيْنَ الرَّجُلِ وَبَيْنَ الْكُفْرِ تَرْكُ الصَّلَاةِ | Between a man and disbelief is abandoning the prayer. | Sahih Muslim
مَن صَلَّى الْبَرْدَيْنِ دَخَلَ الْجَنَّةَ | Whoever prays the two cool prayers (Fajr and Asr) will enter Paradise. | Sahih al-Bukhari
الدُّعَاءُ مُخُّ الْعِبَادَةِ | Supplication is the essence of worship. | Tirmidhi
التَّطَهُّرُ شَطْرُ الْإِيمَانِ | Purity is half of faith. | Sahih Muslim
احْفَظْ اللَّهَ يَحْفَظْكَ | Be mindful of Allah and He will protect you. | Tirmidhi
سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ وَبِحَمْدِهِ تَمْلَأُ الْمِيزَانَ | "Glory be to Allah and praise Him" fills the scales. | Sahih Muslim
خَيْرُ الْأَعْمَالِ أَدْوَمُهَا وَإِنْ قَلَّ | The best deeds are those done consistently, even if few. | Sahih al-Bukhari
